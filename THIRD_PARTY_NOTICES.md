## External detailed mechanisms and models

CRECK/Ranzi, Bio-CPD/CPD, Cellulose V23, detailed gas-phase biomass chemistry, detailed/reduced polymer mechanisms and other literature models may be discussed in catalogues or validation records. Their presence does **not** mean that third-party mechanism files, source code or complete datasets are redistributed in v0.2.0. Legitimate source access and original licence terms remain controlling.

## Scientific claim boundary

Source citation or software availability does not upgrade evidence status. Source reproduction, same-study holdout, independent transfer, cross-study comparison, measured-component baselines and diagnostic assessments remain distinct. Software tests establish implementation integrity, not experimental validation.
